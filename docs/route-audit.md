# Route audit (report only, nothing changed). 2026-11-21

Generated by scanning each `page.tsx` (and its nearest layout) for its first `max-w-*` class and for any font setup, then checked on the live site for the font finding. Widths are the first container class found, so treat them as a guide, not a measurement.

## Finding 1 (confirmed on production): most pages render in the browser's default serif

The Tailwind sans stack points at `--font-dm-sans`, which nothing defines (see `components/landing/landing.css`). Only pages that opt in (`lpl` + `studioFonts`) get the site's sans-serif face: the home page, `/connect`, `/docs/*`, `/oauth/*`, and the dashboard card. Verified by screenshot: `/terms` and `/login` are serif. Likely affected, not all screenshotted: `/blog/*`, `/create/*`, `/privacy`, `/reset-password`, `/invite/[token]`, `/bootcamp/[code]`, `/preview/[id]`, `/portfolio-for/*`, `/portfolio-builder`, `/free-portfolio-website`, `/cv-to-portfolio`, `/what-is-a-portfolio`, `/checkout/return`.

Proposed fix (needs your approval): one global default in `app/layout.tsx` (wrap `<body>` content in the same `lpl` + `studioFonts` class, or set the body font in `globals.css`). One change, every page. Check the dashboard and the portfolio templates afterwards, because templates set their own fonts and must not change.

## Finding 2: container widths are inconsistent

| Group | Routes | First max-w |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow forms | `/login` (lg), `/create` (lg), `/dashboard` (lg), `/oauth/*` (md to xl), `/admin`, `/preview/[id]`, `/bootcamp/[code]` (md), `/invite/[token]` (sm), `/reset-password` (xl) | sm to xl |
| Articles | `/blog/*` (2xl, index 3xl), `/connect` (2xl) | 2xl to 3xl |
| SEO landing pages | `/portfolio-for/*`, `/portfolio-builder`, `/free-portfolio-website`, `/cv-to-portfolio`, `/what-is-a-portfolio` | 4xl |
| Legal | `/terms`, `/privacy` | 5xl |
| Docs | `/docs/*` | 6xl (with sidebar) |

Proposed standard: forms `max-w-md`, reading pages `max-w-3xl`, landing pages `max-w-5xl`, docs `max-w-6xl`. The legal pages (5xl) are wider than a comfortable reading line; 3xl would read better.

## Finding 3: duplicate or overlapping pages

- `/connect` and `/docs/connect` (and `/docs/connect/*`): same job at two addresses. Proposed: keep `/connect` as the marketing and quick-start page, make it link to `/docs/connect/*` for steps, and drop the step text from `/connect`.
- `/portfolio-builder`, `/free-portfolio-website`, `/cv-to-portfolio`, `/what-is-a-portfolio` and `/portfolio-for/*` target overlapping searches. Not a bug; consider internal linking and distinct titles so they do not compete.
- `/portfolio/[slug]` and `/{slug}`: intentional (short link rewrite in `middleware.ts`).
- `/create` and `/create/account`: `/create` redirects to `/create/account` when `NEXT_PUBLIC_ONBOARDING_V2=true`. Intentional.
- `/docs/mcp` (tools reference) and `/llms.txt`: different audiences; fine.

Mobile behaviour of each page, and the dashboard's own internal layout (a single large page).
